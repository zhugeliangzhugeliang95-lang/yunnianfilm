V6 YUNNIAN CATALOGUE — 2026-11-07
User-supplied source: https://wtuce.com/u/YUNNIAN/
Page title: 云辇TPU电子色卡 - 微图册.
Imported all 404 visible JPEG colour-card files in the supplied directory. This is a file count, not a verified count of unique product colours or current stock. Variant markers and source filenames are preserved. Hidden folders were not imported. Original photos and thumbnails are served locally; source URLs remain available from each card.

The 390 enabled base-colour previews use an uncalibrated RGB estimate sampled from the wrap roll in each source photograph. No spectrophotometer, measured spectral data, physical sample matching or camera/light colour calibration was available. A single RGB value does not reproduce multi-angle colour shift, pearl flakes, texture or retroreflection. The 11 carbon/texture and 3 reflective entries remain original-image-only in this version. Chrome and metallic parameters are approximate preview choices. Effect and finish groups are inferred from filenames for browsing, not a verified manufacturer taxonomy. Unmarked finishes default to a gloss simulation; manual finish controls are exploratory and do not establish product availability.

The downloadable native Blender project remains V4 with its previous demonstration materials; the catalogue integration is currently in the website only. Source metadata and the importer report are saved under source/yunnian-catalog/.

V14 WEBSITE MATERIAL STUDY — 2026-11-12
The website now has 404 fixed catalog references and 402 enabled previews; no free color or finish combinations are offered. The thin flexible sample and release liner are newly authored parametric meshes, replacing the previous curved sample panel in the current viewer. The PPF layers use separate thin curved sheets as a generic, not-to-scale diagram, with alpha transparency for legibility rather than measured transmission. The default neutral appearance lighting and alternate HDR observation mode are display approximations; RGB references remain uncalibrated source-photo estimates. No exact multi-angle color-shift or retroreflection measurement is claimed. The downloadable native Blender project remains V4.

V15 OUTDOOR LIGHTING — 2026-11-12
Rooftop Day HDRI — Greg Zaal / Poly Haven, CC0.
https://polyhaven.com/a/rooftop_day ; https://polyhaven.com/license
4K on desktop, 2K on small screens. These are linear-radiance lighting data,
not ordinary product images; product photos and exported looks remain WebP.
Three.js r180 GroundedSkybox.js is vendored from the official MIT source.
The captured world supplies the backdrop and all material reflections. Bright
sun radiance is separated from the IBL into a shadow-casting directional light.
HDR ground projection and realtime shadows are approximations, not a full 3D
reconstruction of the location. No Forza code, shaders, assets or trademarks
are included in the website. Playground's public lighting/material talks were
used as research references, not a claim of game-engine parity.
Body-panel normals are area-weighted with a crease threshold; the hood receives
bounded cubic surface interpolation while its boundary edges remain intact.
The original car asset, product colors, catalogue references and finish choices
are retained. Metallic grain is illustrative, not measured flake geometry.
The downloadable native Blender project remains V4.
